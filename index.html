About VAANI

Why voice authenticity and scam detection need to be solved together.

What it does

VAANI takes a phone-call recording and answers two questions at once: does this voice sound like a real person, and does the conversation follow the pattern of a scam call. Most tools only check one. VAANI reports both on a single dashboard, so a bank, telecom operator or individual can see the full picture before acting.

Why AI voice scams are dangerous

Voice-cloning tools now need only a few seconds of sample audio to generate a convincing fake of someone's voice. Combined with caller-ID spoofing, this is already being used for "relative in trouble" calls, fake bank-verification calls and OTP-theft scripts — and it defeats the one thing people used to trust: hearing a familiar voice on the line.

How the system works

The prototype runs a recording through six stages: extracting the voice track, scoring it against known markers of synthetic speech (pitch variance, spectral texture, naturalness), then separately scanning the conversation for scam language such as urgency, OTP requests or impersonation. The two scores are combined into one risk level.

Where this fits
